Assumptions & direction

OrbitU is a two-sided, student-only marketplace for summer sublets around Seattle University — the June–September gap in a 12-month student lease. Seekers are interns and summer-session students; subletters are the tenants leaving town. Both sides enter through the same door, so the homepage is balanced and the term window (not "anytime") is the primary filter.

Trust is the product: .edu verification gates the whole network, landlord approval is a first-class object on every listing (not fine print), and date matching is treated as a real constraint — a partial-window match is shown as a gap, not hidden. AI does two jobs only: ranking by campus/commute fit, and roommate compatibility. It never invents facts; every AI surface cites what it matched on.

Built on the Nocturne system — dark blue-grey ground, outlined primary actions, Inter, accent used as line and glow. Photography is placeholdered (striped blocks) for you to drop real Seattle unit photos into. Below: three homepage directions, then eight core screens at desktop and mobile.
